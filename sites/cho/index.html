The chef

Six years in Bahrain.
One counter in Singapore.

Before Cho, Chef YK Chong led the kitchen at Bushido, an award-winning Japanese restaurant in Bahrain. Cho is the room he built to cook the way he wants to: a short counter, seasonal fish, and courses paced to the guest in front of him.

There is no à la carte. You choose a menu, sit down, and let the chef take it from there.
